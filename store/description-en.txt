★ TWITCH, BUT BETTER! ★

You know what your Twitch sidebar is missing? All those little bits of info you'd love to have WITHOUT clicking everywhere. ★ COWLOR'S SIDEBAR ★ adds them right where you're already looking. Simple, sleek, and oh-so-useful!

_______________________________

✨ WHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES:

_______________________________

➤ 📺 A LIVE PREVIEW ON HOVER
The flagship feature. Hover a streamer for one second and boom, a popup opens on the side with:

- The LIVE stream video (actual live feed, not a frozen thumbnail)
- The full stream title, readable before you even click
- Badges that tell you everything about the context (see right below)
- And it plays on labelled channels too: Twitch's "Start Watching" screen used to leave the preview stuck on a still image — now it is lifted, and the labels move into a badge (see below)

And it works everywhere in the sidebar: Followed Channels, Recommended Channels, and even "Viewers of X also watch". No more clicking just to find out what's happening on a stream!

_______________________________

➤ 🏷️ BADGES THAT TELL YOU EVERYTHING
Inside the preview popup, colored labels sum up the stream's context at a glance:

- 🤝 ★ CO-STREAM ★ Tells you precisely whether the channel is the host or a guest, and with whom. No more "wait, who is this stream I didn't follow?".
- 👥 ★ LIVE WITH ★ For squads and multistreams, shows the main co-streamer and total count of others ("Live with So-and-So and 3 others").
- 💜 ★ SUBSCRIBED ★ How long you've been supporting the channel ("Subscribed • 9 MONTHS") — or that you used to ("Former sub • 22 MONTHS").
- 💰 ★ SPONSORED BY ★ For sponsored streams, displays the brand with its color logo. You'll know who's behind it.
- 🚂 ★ HYPE TRAIN ★ A Hype Train is running on the channel? You're notified without having to click.
- 💎 ★ DISCOUNTED SUB ★ When Twitch is offering a discounted subscription on the stream, it's flagged right there.
- 🆕 ★ JUST SWITCHED TO ★ The streamer changed category mid-stream? A green badge says so for ten minutes: "Just switched to Elden Ring". Twitch announces it nowhere — the extension sees it because it keeps watching.
- 🔁 ★ BACK AFTER A DROP ★ The streamer dropped and came back? Twitch resets their uptime to zero and says nothing about it. Here the card keeps showing the WHOLE stream's duration, outage included — the badge says what happened, and the "just went live" bar does not light up on a stream that has been running for six hours ("Back after a drop").
- ⚠️ ★ CONTENT LABELS ★ The official Twitch labels (mature-rated game, graphic violence, gambling…) are shown as their own badge, first in the row — so you know before you look, without the interstitial that used to block the video.
- 🌈 ★ SUBATHON ★ The channel is running a subathon? The badge says so and gives the current day ("Subathon • DAY 10").

_______________________________

➤ 🎞️ "PREVIOUSLY ON THIS STREAM"
You walk in halfway through an eight-hour stream. What happened before? The preview shows you: a colored ribbon, one color per category, and the list underneath.

- ★ THE WHOLE JOURNEY ★ Every category played, with how long it lasted and how many times the streamer came back to it ("Just Chatting ×7").
- ★ TO SCALE ★ Each band's width is real time. One glance tells you where the evening went.
- ★ EVEN WITHOUT A REPLAY ★ A channel that doesn't archive? The extension reconstructs the journey from the clips, and SAYS SO.
- ★ IT ADMITS WHAT IT DOESN'T KNOW ★ When the exact time of a switch isn't known, the color fades instead of cutting sharp, and the duration carries a "~". Never an invented number.

_______________________________

➤ 🌈 SUBATHONS, SPOTTED ON THEIR OWN
A subathon is that stream subscriptions keep extending without end — days, sometimes weeks. The extension recognizes them and tells them apart from everything else.

- ★ THE DAY, AGAINST THE NAME ★ A small "D9" pill next to the username: you know at once where the event stands.
- ★ A COLOR THAT BELONGS TO NOBODY ★ Pill and badge run through a rainbow, because an exceptional event doesn't file into anyone else's palette.
- ★ NOTHING GUESSED ★ It's the stream title and the tags that say so, not the duration: a subathon in its second hour already is one.

_______________________________

➤ ⏱️ "HOW LONG HAS HE BEEN STREAMING?"
No need to go to the stream anymore! The extension shows the live duration directly in the sidebar, right under the viewer count.

- 4h19, 14h29… you see it at a glance.
- It updates automatically, in real time.
- And when a stream ends, the counter switches to a subtle italic "Ended" before Twitch even removes the streamer from the list.

_______________________________

➤ 🎯 CO-STREAMS FLAGGED BY A COLOURED BAR
A coloured bar runs down the left edge of the card of every streamer in a co-stream, one colour per co-stream — even when it is the only one you follow. Several members one after another in the list? Their bars join into one. No hover needed, it's visible right in the sidebar, and no number stuck on the avatar any more. Clean, discreet, and it tells you instantly "ah, these ones are streaming together".

_______________________________

➤ 🎬 THE MULTISTREAM ROOM: THE WHOLE CO-STREAM, SIDE BY SIDE
A co-stream is several views of the same moment. The "▶" cursor on its coloured bar opens them all together, side by side, without leaving the page.

- ★ IT SAYS WHAT IT OPENS ★ Hover it: "Watch all 3". Members you don't follow? Their faces show at its end: move onto them to add them, or Shift+click to open the whole co-stream at once.
- ★ THE SOUND WHERE YOU WANT IT ★ Click a tile to give it the sound — keys 1 to 6 work too — and the chat follows the sound.
- ★ LINED UP ON THE SAME MOMENT ★ Each stream arrives with its own delay: the extension lines them up by itself on their common sound, in about a minute of listening. With no common sound, it leaves them alone.
- ★ SIX ON SCREEN ★ Six streams at most; the others wait below, and a click brings them in.
- ★ NOTHING HIDDEN ★ The room earns no channel points, and Twitch's ads play there as anywhere else.

Escape closes it — or a click on the cursor, now a "■".

➤ 💜 YOUR SUBSCRIPTIONS, IN GOLD
The channels you actually support shouldn't look like all the others. Now they don't:

- ★ THE WHOLE CARD LIGHTS UP ★ Golden name, golden category, a gold ring around the avatar, and a slow shimmer in the background of the card. Discreet, but you can't miss it.
- ★ A SORT OF THEIR OWN ★ "My subscriptions first" pushes them all to the top of the list, with your total on a little badge.
- ★ EVERY KIND COUNTS ★ Paid, gifted, mobile: all three are recognized, and all three wear the same gold.
- ★ HOW LONG YOU'VE BEEN THERE ★ Hover a channel and the preview tells you: "Subscribed • 9 MONTHS". And for one you supported in the past: "Former sub • 22 MONTHS".
- ★ IT FIGURES IT OUT ALONE ★ Nothing to configure, no login, no password, no token. How exactly? It's spelled out in the PRIVACY section further down, in plain words.

The extension refreshes all of it itself, every 30 seconds — viewers, category, language, uptime, appearing and disappearing. You see it before Twitch does.

- 🌍 ★ THE GLOBAL RANKING ★ Recomputed continuously, not a frozen list.

Six buttons at the top of the sidebar to rearrange everything on the fly:

- 👁️ ★ BY VIEWERS ★ Twitch's default sort, clean.
- 💎 ★ MY SUBSCRIPTIONS FIRST ★ The channels you support, grouped at the top, with your total on a badge.
- ⭐ ★ BY PERSONAL POPULARITY ★ Based on your own local visit history. Your favorite streamers rise to the top.
- ⏰ ★ BY STREAM DURATION ★ Ascending: just-started streams sit at the top, perfect for catching beginnings.
- 🔤 ★ ALPHABETICAL ★ By channel name. Classic but effective.
- 🔗 ★ CO-STREAMS FIRST ★ All co-streams grouped at the top of the list. Never miss a collab again!

_______________________________

➤ 📊 YOUR TOP STREAMERS, LOCALLY
The extension remembers which channels you visit, when, and how often. This data powers the "Personal Popularity" sort and stays stored ONLY in your browser. Nothing leaves your machine.

You can check your top in the DevTools console (F12):
- tse.scores()       → your top streamers
- tse.scores.raw()   → raw data
- tse.subs()         → the subscriptions the extension has spotted
- tse.subs.refresh() → re-read them right now
- tse.reset()        → wipe everything

➤ 📅 WHO STREAMED, WHO'S LIVE, WHO'S NEXT
A channel's schedule page on Twitch shows one channel. The panel's "Schedule" group shows all the channels you follow, at once.

- ★ THE WEEK, DAY BY DAY ★ It opens on today, the past days above: every past stream with its replay one click away, the ones live right now, and the ones channels have scheduled, each on the day it started. Eight weeks back, two ahead.
- ★ OFFLINE, NOT FORGOTTEN ★ Every followed channel that isn't live: those with a stream scheduled come first, the soonest on top, then the others, the most recent first — when it last streamed, its replay, and its next stream.
- ★ ONE CHANNEL OR ALL ★ A menu keeps a single channel, and "Their week" jumps straight to the week of its last stream.

- ★ ONLY WHEN YOU LOOK ★ Nothing is asked of Twitch until you open it, and what it has read stays in your browser, so a reload picks up where it left off.

➤ 🔒 PRIVACY PEACE OF MIND

- ★ No third-party server ★ The extension only talks to Twitch's own public API — the very one the page already uses to render itself. Nothing else, nowhere.
- ★ Anonymous calls ★ No session token, no cookie is ever sent: those requests cannot be tied back to your account.
- ★ Two exceptions, said out loud ★ To know which channels you subscribe to, the extension opens YOUR own twitch.tv/subscriptions page in an invisible frame, every six hours by default (three, twelve or twenty-four depending on your setting), sooner only if the previous sweep found nothing or if you click "Sweep now", and never when you're signed out. To know ALL the channels you follow — Twitch's sidebar shows only a hundred —, it opens your Following page the same way, every twelve hours, sooner only if the previous reading did not reach the end of the list. Your browser authenticates those pages with its own cookies, exactly as if you had clicked the link: the extension never reads your token and never passes it on. What it keeps — channel names and a tenure in months — stays in your browser.
- ★ No tracker, no pixel, no telemetry ★
- ★ No shady permissions ★ The extension ONLY runs on twitch.tv.
- ★ Ad blocking stays in its lane ★ It applies to the hover preview thumbnail and to nothing else. The stream you're actually watching is untouched.
- ★ Your history stays with you ★ Visits, subscriptions, months, the schedule: stored in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB), period. Clear twitch.tv site data and it's all gone.
- ★ Full source code readable ★ Right in the extension folder. No black box.
