Twitch's sidebar tells you who is live. It doesn't tell you for how long, with
whom, or what is happening on the stream. This extension does — no account, no
setup, no ads.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT YOU GET TO SEE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ Stream uptime on every card — how long the channel has been live.
★ Hover preview — one second, and the stream really opens, with its full title
  and context. No preroll ads.
★ Colour-coded co-streams — channels streaming together share a colour, with the
  role (host or guest) read from the page.
★ Streams that just went live, marked with an edge.
★ Hype Trains and sub-discount banners hidden, sponsored cards normalised.
★ Offline channels and empty sections hidden, followed list expanded on its own.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YOUR SUBSCRIPTIONS, RECOGNISED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ Gilded name, gilded category, a gold ring around the avatar, and a quiet
  animation in the card's background.
★ A "My subscriptions first" sort, with the total on a badge.
★ In the preview, a "Subscribed 9 months" badge — or "Formerly subscribed 22
  months" for a channel you supported in the past.
★ Paid, gifted, mobile: all three count, and all three wear the same gold.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FIND IT, SORT IT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ Filters by category and by language, with flags. Only the values actually
  present among your followed channels are offered — nothing to configure.
★ Six sorts: viewers, subscriptions, personal popularity, stream uptime,
  alphabetical, co-streams grouped.
★ "Top Channels" mode: the 30 most-watched channels on Twitch, globally, by
  category or by language. Twitch does not return that ranking; the extension
  rebuilds it, and says so when it cannot prove it.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
QUICKER THAN TWITCH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Twitch takes 2 to 4 minutes to show a followed channel that just went live, and
5 to 10 minutes to remove one that just stopped. The extension refreshes all of
it itself, every 30 seconds: viewers, category, language, uptime, appearance and
disappearance.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIVACY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ No permissions beyond Twitch. The extension can only act on twitch.tv and
  player.twitch.tv, and on nothing else.
★ No third-party server. No account, no telemetry, no ads, no tracker, no data
  ever sold. Nothing leaves your machine.
★ Calls to Twitch's API are anonymous: public Client-ID, no session token and no
  cookie sent.
★ One exception, and here it is. To learn which channels you subscribe to, the
  extension opens YOUR own twitch.tv/subscriptions page in an invisible frame,
  at most once every six hours, and never if you are not signed in. It is your
  browser that authenticates that page with its own cookies, exactly as if you
  clicked the link: the extension does not read your token and passes it to no
  one. What it keeps — channel names and a tenure in months — stays in your
  browser's local storage.
★ Ad blocking applies ONLY to the hover preview thumbnail. The stream you are
  actually watching is left alone.
★ It all clears at once: clear twitch.tv site data from your browser settings.

COMPATIBILITY

Chrome 111 or newer, and any recent Chromium browser: Edge, Brave, Opera,

Interface in English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil and
Portugal), detected automatically. The extension works whatever language Twitch
